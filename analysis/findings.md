# Population analysis

Analyzed 250 records across 10 countries. Growth could be calculated for 10 countries with both endpoint years.

1. **Population over time.** The trend chart uses a log scale because country populations differ greatly in size. In 2024, the sample ranged from 20,592,571 (Kazakhstan) to 1,450,935,791 (India). Kazakhstan went from 15,501,103 to 20,592,571 people (+32.85%).

2. **Percentage change.** Nigeria recorded the largest change (+84.11%), and Japan recorded the smallest (-2.26%).

3. **Ranking in 2024.** India ranked first among the sampled countries, with 1,450,935,791 people.
