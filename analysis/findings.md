# Population analysis

Analyzed 250 records across 10 countries (2000-2024). Growth could be calculated for 10 countries with both endpoint years.

## Chart 1: Population over time (01_population_trends.png)

Each line is one country. A log scale is used because populations differ by almost two orders of magnitude, so on a linear axis small countries would look flat. In 2024 the sample ranged from 20,592,571 (Kazakhstan) to 1,450,935,791 (India). Parallel lines mean similar relative growth; a flat or falling line means stagnation or decline.

## Chart 2: Population change (02_population_growth.png)

Bars show the percentage change between 2000 and 2024. Nigeria grew the most (+84.11%) and Japan the least (-2.26%). Countries with a decline: Japan, Russian Federation.

## Chart 3: Ranking in 2024 (03_population_ranking.png)

India ranked first with 1,450,935,791 people. The ranking shows absolute size, while chart 2 shows speed of change: a large country can grow slowly in percent but still add the most people.

## Grouped analysis

By region, the largest total population in 2024 is in East Asia & Pacific (1,532,950,371). Details are in population_by_region_income.csv, descriptive_statistics.csv and categorical_statistics.csv.

## Conclusions

Population sizes in the sample are very uneven, growth rates differ strongly between countries, and absolute size does not predict growth speed.

Kazakhstan went from 15,501,103 to 20,592,571 people (+32.85%).
